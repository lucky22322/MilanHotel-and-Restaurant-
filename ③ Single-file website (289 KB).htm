01 — The Craft

Where centuries-old recipes meet Swiss-grade precision.

Every batch is weighed on calibrated mills, tempered in copper-clad kadhais, and rested in climate-mapped chambers. The hand is still the artist's — the dial is now an engineer's.

  • 01
    Single-origin dairy. Buffalo milk from one cooperative in Haringhata, unhomogenised.
  • 02
    Date-tree jaggery. Nolen gur tapped between November and January.
  • 03
    Traceable saffron. Pampore, hand-sorted, lot-stamped.
  • 04
    Same-day dispatch. 2°C cold-chain, never frozen.
